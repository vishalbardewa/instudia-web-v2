## Why Tally Skills Are Always in Demand

Since the GST rollout in 2017, every registered business in India needs someone who understands GST compliance, filing, and accounting software. Tally Prime (the latest version) has become the default accounting solution for SMBs across the country — including right here in Dimapur.

Walk into any business district and ask — nearly every shop, clinic, or firm needs someone who knows Tally.

## What the Course Covers

Our 3-month Tally + GST program is built for practical, on-the-job readiness:

- GST fundamentals — types, slabs, input tax credit, filing
- Tally Prime — company setup, vouchers, ledgers, journals
- GST returns — GSTR-1, GSTR-3B filing in Tally
- Payroll management and TDS basics
- Financial statement generation — Balance Sheet, P&L

## Career Outcomes

Graduates from our accounting program typically find placement as junior accountants, billing executives, or GST assistants. Starting salaries in Dimapur range from ₹8,000–₹18,000/month, with significant scale as experience grows.

Many students also go on to support their family businesses or launch accounting freelance practices.
